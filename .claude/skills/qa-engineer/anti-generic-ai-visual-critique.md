# Skill: Anti-Generic Visual Critique

Load this skill for an ad hoc visual-quality review of a UI/product,
acting as a hostile-but-constructive design reviewer — the goal is to
catch a UI that "works" but feels indistinct.

## Detect

generic composition, a predictable hero section, excessive card usage,
excessive rounded surfaces, gradient dependency, decorative noise,
generic/stock-sounding copy, inconsistent visual rhythm, unnecessary
animation, lack of any recognizable visual point of view.

## Deep test

Remove the logo and brand name from the design. Would it still have a
recognizable point of view? If not, recommend structural changes, not
just color/copy tweaks.

## Output

1. What feels generic
2. What feels distinctive
3. Highest-impact fixes
4. Exact changes to make
5. A final score out of 10
